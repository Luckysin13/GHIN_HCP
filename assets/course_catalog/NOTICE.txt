This is a private, personal-use snapshot of course and tee rating records
collected from the USGA National Course Rating Database (NCRDB).

The data is proprietary. The golf-scraper project documentation states that
republication or commercial use requires permission from the USGA. Do not
redistribute this app or its bundled catalog without obtaining the necessary
permission.

The catalog contains tee par, course and bogey ratings, overall slope, and
available front/back-nine rating and slope values. It does not contain
hole-by-hole pars or yardages. Verify rating values against the current
scorecard before posting scores.
